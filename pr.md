### Soroban AMM – Concentrated Liquidity

- **Slippage Protection** – `add_liquidity` now accepts `min_amount_a` and `min_amount_b` to guard against sandwich attacks. The contract reverts with a clear error if the deposited amounts fall short.
- **Position Snapshots** – New `snapshots.rs` module records pool state (reserves, fees, price range) at a specific ledger height. Snapshots are stored under `DataKey::Snapshots` and capped to avoid unbounded growth.
- **Storage Updates** – `DataKey` enum extended with `Snapshots` and `StakedPositions` variants.
- **Tests** – Unit tests cover slippage failures, successful snapshot creation, and storage caps. All tests pass (`cargo test`).
